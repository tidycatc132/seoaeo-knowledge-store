Summary: Search Console help on manual actions: issued by human reviewers, most spam is caught automatically, affected pages rank lower or are omitted, and review is requested after all issues are fixed.

## Passages

### P1 what
> Google issues a manual action against a site when a human reviewer at Google has determined

### P2 mostly automatic
> in most cases we automatically discover it and remove it from our search results

### P3 effect
> Most issues reported here will result in pages or sites being ranked lower or omitted from search results

### P4 request review
> When all issues listed in the report are fixed in all pages, select Request Review in this report.

### P5 timing
> Most reconsideration reviews can take several days or weeks
