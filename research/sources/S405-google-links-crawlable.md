### P1 acrawl
> Google can only crawl your link if it's an `<a>` HTML element
### P2 anchor
> Good anchor text is descriptive, reasonably concise, and relevant to the page that it's on

### P4 internal
> Every page you care about should have a link from at least one other page on your site.
### P5 nohref
> Google can't reliably extract URLs from `<a>` elements that don't have an `href` attribute
