Summary: Google guidance on crawlable links, anchor text and internal linking.
Note: quotes returned by WebFetch extraction; fragments kept short.
## Passages
### P1 acrawl
> Google can only crawl your link if it's an `<a>` HTML element
### P2 anchor
> Good anchor text is descriptive, reasonably concise, and relevant to the page that it's on
### P3 cram
> resist the urge to cram every keyword that's related to
### P4 internal
> Every page you care about should have a link from at least one other page on your site.
### P5 nohref
> Google can't reliably extract URLs from `<a>` elements that don't have an `href` attribute

### P6 anchor definition
> Anchor text (also known as link text) is the visible text of a link.

### P7 tells Google
> This text tells people and Google something about the page you're linking to.

### P8 bad example
> Click here

### P9 better example
> list of cheese types

### P10 words around links
> the words before and after links matter

### P11 no chaining
> Don't chain up links next to each other

### P12 image alt
> For images as links, Google uses the alt attribute of the img element as anchor text,

### P13 alt text
> so be sure to add descriptive alt text to your images.

### P14 internal resources
> Think about what other resources on your site could help your readers understand a given page on your site

### P15 external links
> Linking to other sites isn't something to be scared of; in fact, using external links can help establish trustworthiness
