Summary: How Business Profile verification works; ownership is gained on verification; methods vary by business.
## Passages
### P1 ownership
> When you verify your business on Google, you gain ownership of your Business Profile
### P2 postcard
> Verification through postcard isn’t available for all businesses.
### P3 video
> If your business is eligible for video verification, we recommend that you use this option.
### P4 methods fixed
> Verification methods are automatically determined by Google and can’t be changed.
### P5 phone or text
> To get your verification code, make sure you can answer your business phone number or receive text messages.
### P6 email
> Make sure you can use the email address in the verification screen.
### P7 live video
> Live video verification may not be available for all businesses.
### P8 review time
> verification reviews take up to 5 business days.
### P9 confirmation
> When your business is verified, you’ll get a confirmation email.
