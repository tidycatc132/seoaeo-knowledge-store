Summary: Google's 2012 launch post for the Knowledge Graph. Describes entities and relationships, the graph's size at launch, and the Taj Mahal disambiguation example. Scale figures are as of 2012.
## Passages

### P1 what it is
> The Knowledge Graph enables you to search for things, people or places that Google knows about

### P2 things not strings
> understands real-world entities and their relationships to one another: things, not strings

### P3 objects
> currently contains more than 500 million objects

### P4 facts
> more than 3.5 billion facts about and relationships between these different objects

### P5 two words
> To a search engine the words [taj mahal] have been just that—two words

### P6 disambiguation
> Now Google understands the difference, and can narrow your search results just to the one you mean
