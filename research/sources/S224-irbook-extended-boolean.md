Summary: Textbook section contrasting Boolean and ranked retrieval: Boolean is precise and gives control, AND favours precision, OR favours recall, proximity operators, and Westlaw's experience.
## Passages
### P1 precise
> Boolean queries are precise: a document either matches the query or it does not.
### P2 control
> This offers the user greater control and transparency over what is retrieved.
### P3 AND
> using AND operators tends to produce high precision but low recall searches
### P4 OR
> using OR operators gives low precision but high recall searches
### P5 middle ground
> it is difficult or impossible to find a satisfactory middle ground.
### P6 proximity
> A proximity operator is a way of specifying that two terms in a query must occur close to each other in a document
### P7 professionals
> Many users, particularly professionals, prefer Boolean query models.
### P8 westlaw test
> free text queries produced better results than Boolean queries prepared by Westlaw's own reference librarians
