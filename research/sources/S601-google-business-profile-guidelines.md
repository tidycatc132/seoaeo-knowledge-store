Summary: Google's eligibility and representation guidelines: real-world business name, accurate address, few specific categories, service-area rules.
## Passages
### P1 physical location
> If your business either has a physical location that customers can visit
### P2 address
> P.O. boxes or mailboxes located at remote locations aren't acceptable.
### P3 categories
> Select categories that complete the statement: "This business IS a"
### P4 sab hide address
> Service-area businesses should hide their business address from customers.
### P5 service area limit
> The boundaries of your profile's overall service area shouldn't extend farther than about 2 hours of driving time from where
### P6 name
> Your name should reflect your business's real-world name
### P7 unnecessary info
> Including unnecessary information in your business name isn't permitted
### P8 no taglines
> Marketing taglines
### P9 no store codes
> Store codes
### P10 travels to customers
> or travels to customers where they are
### P11 precise address
> Use a precise, accurate address and/or service area to describe your business location.
### P12 suite numbers
> Suite numbers, floors, building numbers, and so on may also be included.
### P13 virtual office
> Service-area businesses can't list a "virtual" office unless that office is staffed during business hours.
### P14 hours
> Provide your regular customer-facing hours of operation.
### P15 few categories
> Use as few categories as possible to describe your overall core business from the provided list.
### P16 specific categories
> Choose categories that are as specific as possible, but representative of your main business.
### P17 not keywords
> Do not use categories solely as keywords or to describe attributes of your business.
### P18 most specific
> Focus primarily on adding the most specific categories for your business; we'll do the rest behind the scenes.
### P19 one profile
> should have one profile for the central office or location with a designated service area
