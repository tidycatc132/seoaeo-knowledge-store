Summary: Google's Maps content policy bans fake engagement, paid reviews and conflict-of-interest content.
## Passages
### P1 fake engagement
> Fake engagement is not allowed and will be removed.
### P2 paid
> Reviews or ratings that have been paid for, directly or in kind.
### P3 conflict
> Content that is based on a conflict of interest.
### P4 list heading
> Fake Engagement
### P5 list intro
> This includes:
### P6 fake engagement includes paid
> Fake engagement is not allowed and will be removed. This includes: Reviews or ratings that have been paid for, directly or in kind.
### P7 genuine
> Contributions to Google Maps should reflect a genuine experience at a place or business.
### P8 multiple accounts
> Content that has been posted from multiple accounts by or at the request of one person.
### P9 solicit
> Solicit or encourage the posting of content that does not represent a genuine experience.
### P10 incentives
> Offer incentives – such as payment, discounts, free goods and/or services - in exchange for posting any review
### P11 incentives continued
> or revision or removal of a negative review.
### P12 competitors
> Post content on a competitor’s place or business to undermine that business’ or product’s reputation.
### P13 selective
> Discourage or prohibit negative reviews, or selectively solicit positive reviews from customers.
### P14 conflict employment
> A conflict of interest may include current or former employment, a contractual or consultory relationship,
### P15 conflict examples
> (such as industry competitors, familial relationships, etc.).
