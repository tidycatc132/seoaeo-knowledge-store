Summary: Secondary report of Google's May 2023 post on its news topic authority system: it helps find expert sources for newsy queries in specialised topic areas, publishers need do nothing special, and Danny Sullivan said it had been used for several years. Applies to news, not to a general site score.
## Passages

### P1 purpose
> helps determine which expert sources are helpful to someone's newsy query

### P2 topic areas
> in certain specialized topic areas, such as health, politics, or finance

### P3 relevant to topics or locations
> especially relevant to topics or locations

### P4 nothing special
> just do what you are doing today, as you normally would

### P5 coverage
> Provide great coverage about the areas and topics they know well

### P6 not new
> this isn't a new system that's just launched. We've used it for several years.
