Summary: Google's local ranking help page. Names relevance, distance and prominence; says reviews, links and complete info matter; says ranking cannot be requested or paid for. Does not mention SEO or website rankings.
## Passages
### P1 relevance
> Relevance is how well a Business Profile matches what someone is searching for.
### P2 distance
> Distance refers to how far each business is from the customer who’s searching.
### P3 prominence
> Prominence means how well-known a business is.
### P4 prominence inputs
> This factor’s also based on info like how many websites link to your business and how many reviews you have.
### P5 reviews
> More reviews and positive ratings can help your business’s local ranking.
### P6 no paying
> There's no way to request or pay for a better local ranking on Google.
### P7 verify
> This tells Google that you’re authorized to represent the business
### P8 complete info
> Businesses with complete and accurate info are more likely to show up in local search results
### P9 intro wording
> Local results are mainly based on relevance, distance, and popularity.
### P10 nearby business
> Google tries to show customers the kind of nearby business they’d like to visit.
### P11 inaccurate info
> If your business info isn’t accurate, your Business Profile might not show up for relevant searches in your area.
### P12 location fallback
> If a customer doesn’t share where they are, Google uses what it knows about their location.
### P13 reviews stand out
> Positive reviews and helpful replies can help your business stand out.
### P14 replies
> When you reply to customer reviews, it shows that you value their feedback.
### P15 where local results appear
> When a customer searches for a business or place near their location, they’ll find local results on Google Maps and Search.
### P16 hours
> Your business hours, including special store hours.
