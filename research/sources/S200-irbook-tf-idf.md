Summary: Textbook section combining tf and idf into one weight. The weight is highest for terms concentrated in few documents and lowest for terms in nearly all.
## Passages
### P1 composite weight
> a composite weight for each term in each document
### P2 highest
> occurs many times within a small number of documents
### P3 lowest
> the term occurs in virtually all documents.
### P4 fewer or many
> the term occurs fewer times in a document, or occurs in many documents
