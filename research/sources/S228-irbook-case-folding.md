Summary: Textbook section on case-folding: lowercasing is common and usually most practical, but some proper nouns differ from common nouns only by case.
## Passages
### P1 case folding
> A common strategy is to do case-folding by reducing all letters to lower case.
### P2 proper nouns
> Many proper nouns are derived from common nouns and so are distinguished only by case
### P3 practical
> Thus, lowercasing everything often remains the most practical solution.
