Summary: Paper proposing GraphRAG: an LLM builds an entity knowledge graph from source documents, then pregenerates community summaries. Aimed at global questions over a corpus. Evidence of a method, not of search engine behaviour.
## Passages
### P1 entity graph
> derive an entity knowledge graph from the source documents
### P2 community summaries
> pregenerate community summaries for all groups of closely related entities.
### P3 motivation
> RAG fails on global questions directed at an entire text corpus
