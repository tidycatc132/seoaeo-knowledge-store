Summary: Original RRF paper: a simple method for fusing rankings from several systems, with k = 60.
## Passages
### P1 rrf
> Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF), a simple method for combining the document rankings from multiple IR systems,
### P2 better
> consistently yields better results than any individual system, and better results than the standard method Condorcet Fuse.
### P3 k60
> where k = 60 was fixed during a pilot investigation and not altered during subsequent validation.
### P4 k role
> The constant k mitigates the impact of high rankings by outlier systems.
