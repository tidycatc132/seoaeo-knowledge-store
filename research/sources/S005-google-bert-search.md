Summary: Google announcement of BERT in Search to interpret queries using word context. Says prepositions like 'to' can change meaning. Applies to about one in ten US English searches at launch.
## Passages
### P1 context
> BERT models can therefore consider the full context of a word by looking at the words that come before and after it
### P2 prepositions
> searches where prepositions like “for” and “to” matter a lot to the meaning
### P3 scale
> BERT will help Search better understand one in 10 searches in the U.S. in English
