Summary: Google guidance on video: eligibility needs an indexed watch page and a valid thumbnail; metadata via structured data or sitemaps.

> To be eligible for video features, a video must meet the following indexing requirements:
### P2 thumbnail
> To be eligible to appear in video features, a video must have a valid thumbnail image.
### P3 metadata
> To make it easier for Google to find your videos, we recommend providing metadata about the video.
### P4 key moments
> Google Search tries to automatically detect the segments in your video
### P5 watch page indexed
> The watch page must be indexed.
### P6 embedded on watch page
> The video must be embedded on a watch page.
### P7 watch page defined
> A watch page's main purpose is to show users a single video.
### P8 where videos appear
> including the main search results page, Video mode, Google Images, and Discover
### P9 video bytes
> Don't block the URL of the actual video bytes with the noindex rule
