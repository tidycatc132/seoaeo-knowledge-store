Summary: Google's own list of notable ranking systems, including RankBrain, neural matching, BERT, MUM, passage ranking and freshness systems. Says it is a guide to some notable systems, not a full list.
## Passages
### P1 many signals
> Google uses automated ranking systems that look at many factors and signals about hundreds of billions of web pages
### P2 RankBrain
> RankBrain is an AI system that helps us understand how words are related to concepts.
### P3 RankBrain exact words
> It means we can better return relevant content even if it doesn't contain all the exact words used in a search,
### P4 BERT
> allows us to understand how combinations of words express different meanings and intent.
### P5 MUM
> It's not currently used for general ranking in Search but rather for some specific applications
### P6 freshness
> We have various "query deserves freshness" systems designed to show fresher content for queries where it would be expected.
### P7 earthquake example
> if an earthquake happened recently, then news articles and fresher content might appear.
### P8 passage ranking
> is an AI system we use to identify individual sections or "passages" of a web page to
### P9 not complete
> This page is a guide to understanding some of our more notable ranking systems.
### P10 multiple systems
> Multiple systems work in various ways to show the most reliable information possible
### P11 neural matching
> Neural matching is an AI system that Google uses to understand representations of concepts in queries
