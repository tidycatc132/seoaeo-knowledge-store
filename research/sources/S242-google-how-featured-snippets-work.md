Summary: Google's plain-language help page for searchers on featured snippets: where they appear, how Google picks them, and that Google takes down snippets that break its policies.

## Passages

### P1 what
> This box shows a little piece of a website that helps answer your question.

### P2 source
> The featured snippets come from websites that Google finds.

### P3 picks
> It picks them based on how well they answer your question and how helpful they are.

### P4 places intro
> You can find these featured snippets:

### P5 top
> At the top of the search results.

### P6 people also ask
> In the "People also ask" section.

### P7 more than one
> Usually, there’s only one box, but sometimes there can be more.

### P8 takes down
> If a featured snippet breaks the rules, Google takes it down.

### P9 consensus policy
> Contradicting consensus on public interest topics

### P10 feedback
> If you give Google feedback, it can learn to find even better answers.
