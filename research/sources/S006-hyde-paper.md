Summary: Research paper (HyDE): an LM writes a hypothetical answer document, which is used to retrieve real documents. Evidence of a method, not of any engine's behaviour.
## Passages
### P1 method
> instructs an instruction-following language model (e.g. InstructGPT) to generate a hypothetical document.
### P2 caveat
> The document captures relevance patterns but is unreal and may contain false details.
