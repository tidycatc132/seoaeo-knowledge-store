## Passages
### P1 searchbot
> OAI-SearchBot is used to surface websites in search results in ChatGPT's search features.
### P2 gptbot
> Disallowing GPTBot indicates a site's content should not be used in training generative AI foundation models.
### P3 independent
> Each setting is independent of the others.
### P4 recommend
> To help ensure your site appears in search results, we recommend allowing OAI-SearchBot in your site's robots.txt file.
### P5 opted out
> Sites that are opted out of OAI-SearchBot will not be shown in ChatGPT search answers.
### P6 delay
> it can take ~24 hours from a site's robots.txt update for our systems to adjust.
