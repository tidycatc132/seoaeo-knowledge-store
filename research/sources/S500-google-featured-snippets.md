Summary: Google documentation on featured snippets: chosen by Google systems, no markup to opt in, opt out via nosnippet / data-nosnippet / max-snippet, no exact minimum length.
## Passages
### P1 definition
> Featured snippets are special boxes where the format of a regular search result is reversed
### P2 chosen
> Google systems determine whether a page would make a good featured snippet
### P3 no minimum
> Google does not provide an exact minimum length required to appear as a featured snippet.
### P4 data-nosnippet
> Text marked by the data-nosnippet HTML attribute won't appear in featured snippets or regular snippets either.
### P5 low max-snippet
> Using a low max-snippet setting doesn't guarantee that Google will stop showing featured snippets for your page.
### P6 enough text
> Featured snippets will only appear if enough text can be shown to generate a useful featured snippet.
### P7 click target
> Clicking a featured snippet takes the user directly to the section of the page that appeared in the featured snippet.
### P8 cannot mark a page
