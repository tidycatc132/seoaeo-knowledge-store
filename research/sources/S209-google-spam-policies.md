Summary: Google's spam policy defines keyword stuffing as filling a page with keywords to manipulate rankings.
## Passages
### P1 definition
> Keyword stuffing refers to the practice of filling a web page with keywords or numbers in an attempt to manipulate rankings
### P2 unnatural
> Repeating the same words or phrases so often that it sounds unnatural.
