Summary: Replying to and flagging reviews; replying requires a verified business.
## Passages
### P1 reply
> When you reply to their reviews, you show them that you value their feedback.
### P2 verify to reply
> Before you can reply to reviews, you must verify your business.
### P3 flag
> If you believe that a review violates Google's content policies, you can flag it.
### P4 responsive
> Helpful and positive replies to reviews can show that you're responsive to your customers.
### P5 edit if not approved
> If your reply isn't approved to be posted, you'll be asked to edit it.
### P6 review time
> Replies usually take up to 10 minutes to review, but sometimes a review can take up to 30 days.
### P7 posted
> If your reply is approved, it will be publicly posted under the customer’s review.
### P8 as business
> It will appear like your business replied, and your personal name won't be shown.
### P9 notified
> The person who wrote the review is notified when you reply.
### P10 can change
> Customers can still change their review after reading your reply.
