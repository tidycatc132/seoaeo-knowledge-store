Summary: Google says businesses may ask customers for reviews via a link or QR code, but incentives are prohibited and reviews must reflect genuine experience.
## Passages
### P1 ask
> To leave reviews, you can ask customers to visit a Google link or scan a QR code.
### P2 signed in
> Customers must be signed into a Google Account to leave a review.
### P3 incentives
> Offering incentives, like free or discounted goods or services, in exchange for customers to post reviews
### P4 genuine
> Reviews and other user contributions to Google Maps must reflect a genuine experience.
### P5 prohibited
> content and is strictly prohibited
### P6 prohibited full
> is considered fake & misleading content and is strictly prohibited.
### P7 replies public
> Your replies are public and help you build good relationships with customers.
### P8 no deals in replies
> Your reviewers are already customers, so avoid using your response to offer deals or promotions.
### P9 prompt response
> A prompt response shows that you value your customers' feedback and are committed to high-quality service.
