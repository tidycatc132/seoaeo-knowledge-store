Summary: Google's public explainer of ranking. Saved passages state that Google uses aggregated and anonymized interaction data to assess relevance and turns it into signals for machine-learned systems, plus personalisation inputs.
## Passages

### P1 interaction data
> We also use aggregated and anonymized interaction data to assess whether search results are relevant to queries.

### P2 signals
> We transform that data into signals that help our machine-learned systems better estimate relevance.

### P3 personal context
> we use information such as your location, past Search history, and Search settings

### P4 revisits
> Our systems can recognize if you have visited the same page multiple times before
