Summary: Google VP Search describes RankBrain, neural matching, BERT and MUM and how they relate. Says BERT works with other systems and that MUM was not then used to rank.
## Passages
### P1 RankBrain first
> When we launched RankBrain in 2015, it was the first deep learning system deployed in Search.
### P2 neural matching
> Neural matching helps us understand fuzzier representations of concepts in queries and pages
### P3 BERT critical
> Today, BERT plays a critical role in almost every English query.
### P4 BERT not alone
> is never working alone
### P5 MUM not ranking
> MUM is not currently used to help rank and improve the quality of search results

### P6 RankBrain example query
> what's the title of the consumer at the highest level of a food chain

### P7 RankBrain apex predator
> RankBrain understands that you're looking for what's commonly referred to as an "apex predator."

### P8 RankBrain ranks
> used to help rank — or decide the best order for — top search results

### P9 neural matching 2018
> But it wasn't until 2018, when we introduced neural matching to Search

### P10 BERT 2019
> Launched in 2019, BERT was a huge step change in natural language understanding

### P11 BERT ensemble
> like all of our systems, BERT is part of an ensemble of systems that work together

### P12 MUM power
> A thousand times more powerful than BERT, MUM is capable of both understanding and generating language

### P13 MUM multimodal
> MUM is also multimodal, meaning it can understand information across multiple modalities such as text, images

### P14 hundreds of models
> Search runs on hundreds of algorithms and machine learning models

### P15 specialized roles
> Each algorithm and model has a specialized role, and they trigger at different times and in distinct combinations
