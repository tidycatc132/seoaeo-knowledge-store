Summary: Google documentation for Organization markup: administrative details, sameAs links to other pages about the organization, no required properties.
## Passages
### P1 purpose
> You can use Organization markup to let Google know administrative details about your organization
### P2 disambiguation
> Some properties are used behind the scenes to disambiguate your organization from other organizations
### P3 sameAs
> The URL of a page on another website with additional information about your organization, if applicable.
### P4 none required
> There are no required properties; instead, add the properties that apply to your content.

### P5 visual elements
> others can influence visual elements in Search results

### P6 placement
> We recommend placing this information on your home page, or a single page that describes your organization

### P7 not every page
> You don't need to include it on every page of your site.

### P8 logo
> A logo that is representative of your organization, if applicable.

### P9 url
> This helps Google uniquely identify your organization.

### P10 validate
> Validate your code using the Rich Results Test and fix any critical errors.
