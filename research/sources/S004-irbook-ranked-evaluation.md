Summary: Textbook section on precision at k, MAP, R-precision and NDCG. MAP is stable; P@k is least stable; NDCG is for graded relevance.
## Passages
### P1 precision at k
> This leads to measuring precision at fixed low levels of retrieved results, such as 10 or 30 documents.
### P2 P@k stability
> it is the least stable of the commonly used evaluation measures
### P3 MAP
> Mean Average Precision (MAP), which provides a single-figure measure of quality across recall levels.
### P4 MAP stability
> Among evaluation measures, MAP has been shown to have especially good discrimination and stability.
### P5 NDCG graded
> NDCG is designed for situations of non-binary notions of relevance (cf. Section 8.5.1 ).
### P6 PR curve
> For each such set, precision and recall values can be plotted to give a precision-recall curve
