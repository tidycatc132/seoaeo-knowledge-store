Summary: Google's help page lists quotation marks for exact match and the minus sign to exclude a word.
## Passages
### P1 exact
> Enter a word or a phrase inside quotes
### P2 exclude
> Enter `-` in front of a word that you want to leave out.
### P3 site
> Enter site: in front of a site or domain.
### P4 before
> Enter before: in front of a year or specific date.
### P5 filetype
> Enter filetype: in front of the file type.
### P6 no spaces
> Do not put spaces between the operator and your search term.
### P7 minus example
> jaguar speed -car
