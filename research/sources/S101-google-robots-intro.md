Summary: Explains robots.txt as a crawler-traffic tool, not a way to keep pages out of Google. Blocked URLs can still be indexed if linked; use noindex or password protection.
Note: quotes were returned by WebFetch extraction; fragments kept short.
## Passages
### P1 what
> A robots.txt file tells search engine crawlers which URLs the crawler can access on your site.
### P2 purpose
> A robots.txt file is used primarily to manage crawler traffic to your site
### P3 not-hide
> it is not a mechanism for keeping a web page out of Google
### P4 linked
> A page that's disallowed in robots.txt can still be indexed if linked to from other sites.
### P5 noindex
> To keep a web page out of Google, block indexing with noindex or password-protect the page.
